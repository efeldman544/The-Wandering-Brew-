Our story

Four beers in, we have not made the same one twice. That is either discipline or restlessness.

We started brewing in 2023 with one recipe, a lot of opinions and no plan beyond making something we would actually want to drink. King of the Hill came out first, and people kept asking when there would be more.

So there was more. Pour Decisions, a double dry hopped IPA for the choices that look bad on paper. Then The Wandering Brew, a double IPA at 8.7% with a lone figure walking into a nebula — the biggest thing we had attempted, and the one that set the tone for everything after it.

Nothing is repeated and nothing is made in volume. Jerusalem Syndrome is the newest, and the most colourful label we have put on a bottle.
